A newer version: the app says so, once, and waits

Stage 2 of releases, its first half. Matters learns that a newer release exists and says it in one quiet place, away from the controls. It installs nothing: the download opens in the browser, as it does from the site. A newer version is a state of the app, not a message to the person, so there is no banner, no badge and no dialog.

1. In the window: a dot on the Settings gear

The list already has a foot row: the accounts and their sync, the agent's reading, the gear. Nothing is added to it. When a newer version is known the gear carries a dot, and it opens Settings on About.

The dot

  • 7 px, the colour of the text. Green is "sent", red is "late", violet is the agent's.
  • Its words are in the tooltip, in the gear's name for a screen reader, in the menu and in Settings.
  • With the dot the gear opens About. Without it, Settings as today.
  • The same dot marks About in the list of sections.

When it goes

  • Once About has been shown, both dots go for this version, in every window.
  • The next version brings them back. About keeps saying what is out.

What it costs

  • The window says it without words, and a dot on a gear is easy to miss. If testers stay on old versions, the next step is a segment in the foot row.

  • Opens Settings on About and checks now. The answer is the line there, in words, also when it is "newest" or "could not check".
  • A failed check is said only here, never in the mail window: it is not the person's problem to solve.

Words

  • "is out", "newest": the app's plain register. No "update available", no exclamation mark.
  • The stage is always named for a pre-release, as on the site (ADR 0129).

3. What it never does

No system bannerBanners are for mail (ADR 0071). A version is not mail.
No Dock badgeThe badge counts unread mail and nothing else.
No dialog at startNothing stands between the person and their mail.
No install, no download by the appThat is the second half of stage 2, with its own record: it needs a checked signature.
No "skip forever"Seeing About clears the dot for that version only. Turning the check off is in Settings.
